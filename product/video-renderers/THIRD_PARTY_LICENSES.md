# Video renderer licensing

- HyperFrames `0.7.57` is the bundled default renderer under Apache-2.0.
- GSAP `3.14.2` is bundled under GreenSock's Standard “no charge” license.
- Remotion `4.0.488` is bundled for the current MVP validation phase under
  Remotion's special license. Before distributing the customer installation
  package, confirm that the current legal entity remains eligible or obtain a
  company license. Later payment does not retroactively authorize an
  ineligible earlier distribution.

Exact versions are sealed in `version-policy.json`, `package.json` and
`package-lock.json`. The installer refuses version drift.
