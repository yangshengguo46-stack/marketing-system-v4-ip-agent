# Douyin platform evidence

Last reviewed: 2026-07-30. Use current authenticated creator-center evidence
over this static summary whenever they conflict.

## Official evidence

- [Douyin Safety and Trust Center](https://95152.douyin.com/) is the current
  official public entry for recommendation transparency and governance.
- [CAC algorithm filing PDF](https://beian.cac.gov.cn/api/static/fileUpload/principalOrithm/additional/user_9b84b02a-0c7f-4bd4-81f2-5cad879ad4ab_96ed08c8-0ed8-4ab5-b04d-46cccf4c00ab.pdf)
  describes interaction and viewing behavior such as clicks, duration, likes,
  comments, shares and negative feedback; it also describes ranking,
  diversification, intervention and interest exploration. It is an official
  filing, not a current public weight table.
- [Open Platform video-data solution](https://open.douyin.com/platform/resource/docs/ability/open-data/video-data-solution)
  documents authorized post and account data such as likes, shares, comments
  and followers.
- [Open Platform single-video data](https://open.douyin.com/platform/resource/docs/openapi/video-management/douyin/search-video/video-data)
  documents official post-level metric retrieval.

## Diagnosis use

Inspect current recommendation/rule status, content inventory, per-post
viewing and interaction evidence, traffic sources, audience, comments and
commercial outcomes. Separate cumulative snapshots from interval changes.

Do not infer a fixed “cold-start traffic pool,” a universal 500-view threshold,
current signal weights or a shadowban from low views. Official materials
support a multi-objective, personalized and governed system; exact live weights
and allocation thresholds are not public.
