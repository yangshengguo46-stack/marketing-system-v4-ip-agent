# Xiaohongshu platform evidence

Last reviewed: 2026-07-30. Current creator-center and rule evidence outranks
static summaries.

## Official evidence

- [CAC algorithm filing PDF](https://beian.cac.gov.cn/api/static/fileUpload/principalOrithm/additional/user_c015445c-80ac-45f7-94d7-3871e961b1fe_d4425f3b-7f35-45af-b8d4-badd4424d6d5.pdf)
  describes use of note content and behaviors including follow, view, click,
  search, like, save, share, comment and posting, plus deduplication, diversity
  and interest exploration. It does not publish current weights.
- [Creator Service Platform](https://creator.xiaohongshu.com/) is the
  first-party creator analytics and operating entry.
- [Pugongying rules and help](https://pgy.xiaohongshu.com/help/home) is the
  official current commercial-content rule entry.
- [Current content review guidance](https://pgy.xiaohongshu.com/help/detail?id=6495c527d1eedeeb48fb18b1f875650e&userType=4)
  addresses low-information content, misleading titles or topic mismatch, and
  fabricated or mass-produced content.
- [Official topic rules](https://top.xiaohongshu.com/fe/toph5/rules/subject)
  emphasize authentic experience, topic relevance and restrictions on
  meaningless, misleading or engagement-bait content.

## Diagnosis use

Inspect title/cover/content consistency, specificity, proof, originality,
search-query fit, saves, shares, comments, profile actions and commercial
outcomes. A save is useful intent evidence only in context; it is not a sale.

Do not infer current weights, a fixed initial-exposure pool or shadowban from
low exposure. Platform rules and eligibility constrain distribution; content
and audience value remain the primary diagnosis.
