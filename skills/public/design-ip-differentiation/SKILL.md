# IP differentiation design

> Research quarantine (2026-08-01): this method package is not active in the
> default IP Agent. Differentiation storage and tools are retired; references
> below are historical research only. See `IP_AGENT.md`.

Treat IP as a public influence asset: a set of expectations and associations
that a relevant public can attribute to a person, brand, product or
organization, and that can change attention, interpretation, trust, choice or
action over time. Follower count, visual polish, platform reach and a single
viral post are evidence inputs or distribution outcomes, not the asset itself.

Do not jump from biography, product features or a benchmark account to slogans.
The job is to make a consequential choice, prove why it belongs to this entity,
encode it so people can recognize it repeatedly, and test whether that
recognition progresses toward the intended influence and operating outcome.

## Start from the operated entity

1. Call `personal_ip_startup_context`.
2. For a true new owner, answer the current request and learn only the next
   fact that can change entity scope, desired influence, public, choice or
   evidence. Create a creator, brand, product or organization naturally when
   there is enough information; do not inspect empty ledgers.
3. For a returning owner, read the operating cockpit and
   `personal_ip_read_differentiation` for the relevant subject. Reuse prior
   evidence and continue from the first material gap.
4. Distinguish the primary entity from supporting entities. A founder may
   authenticate a product; a product may demonstrate a brand promise; a
   customer community may provide evidence. Do not silently collapse them into
   one personal persona.

## Build the decision

1. Define the desired influence before the content format:
   who should remember, interpret, trust, prefer or act differently; in which
   decision situation; by what horizon; and which adoption or economic outcome
   matters. Influence-first is allowed, but it still needs a plausible path to
   durable adoption, cooperation or monetization.
2. Map the real choice field:
   category, public jobs, direct and indirect alternatives, category minimums,
   competitor territories, clichés and anti-benchmarks. Use real sources.
   Platform mechanics constrain execution but do not become the central reason
   to choose the IP.
3. Mine proprietary truth:
   owned evidence, rare capability, causal mechanism, history, relationships,
   access, rights, product behavior, difficult choices and repeatable operating
   actions. Separate observation, inference and hypothesis.
4. Form enough materially different candidate directions to reveal a real
   choice. When relevant, state:
   value created, meaning created, reason to choose, reason to believe,
   relevance hypothesis, what will deliberately be sacrificed, capacity cost,
   extension boundary and failure condition.
5. Reject adjective-only difference such as “more professional,” “more real,”
   “more premium” or “more cinematic” unless the claim identifies an observable
   mechanism, evidence and choice consequence. Reject a direction that could be
   copied by changing only the name.
6. Recommend one direction with explicit trade-offs. Record it as a
   `candidate` through `personal_ip_record_differentiation`; keep alternatives
   and rejected territories in the private evidence basis.

Read [decision-judgment.md](references/decision-judgment.md) when comparing
directions or deciding whether the difference is real.

## Turn strategy into a repeatable creative system

When the current task benefits from a repeatable creative system, compile the
two engines below. This is a creative aid, not a status gate:

- The dramatic engine defines the recurring protagonist, public desire,
  counterforce, consequential choice, cost and state change, relationship
  pressure, world, event generators, genre/emotional promise and point of view.
  It must generate different real episodes without resetting the entity after
  every post.
- The distinctive encoding system defines verbal, visual, sonic, character,
  spatial/ritual and product-behavior signals. Separate invariants from
  controlled variables, name forbidden combinations and specify the
  attribution target: what should remain recognizable when the logo or name is
  absent.

The system is not a frozen style guide. Stable signals create attribution;
controlled variation prevents fatigue. Cinema craft, hooks and platform
adaptation may strengthen the system but may not replace its strategic choice.

When planning a pilot, record what is actually useful:

- evidence level and immutable evidence references;
- observable hypotheses rather than neuroscience shorthand;
- prediction, observation window, success signal and failure condition;
- capacity, channel and rights constraints;
- what evidence would revise or retire the direction.

Read [thesis-contract.md](references/thesis-contract.md) before writing or
advancing the private version.

## Bind downstream work

- Positioning, name, biography, offer, conversion path and launch pilots should
  use the latest available direction note. A missing note does not block the
  work; state the assumption and avoid silently opening a parallel thesis.
- A series bible inherits the same protagonist, recurring choice, world,
  event generators, emotional promise, invariants and forbidden combinations.
- A script translates one event into observable action and state change. It
  does not re-decide the IP.
- Directing, cinematography, production design, sound, performance and editing
  express the same invariant system while varying the episode.
- Platform-specific work adapts packaging, duration, interaction and
  eligibility without changing the core choice unless observed evidence forces
  a new thesis version.

If downstream work cannot trace its choices to the current note, mark the new
choice as an assumption and continue; update the note later if that choice is
worth retaining.

## Observe, advance or retire

After real publication, research, product use or commercial activity, seal
credential-free observations with `personal_ip_record_asset_observation`.
Record what was actually observed and keep missing or partial coverage explicit.

Use these effect classes:

- recognition: correct attribution or recall;
- trust: specific evidence-based confidence, not generic praise;
- intent: qualified inquiry, evaluation or stated preference;
- adoption: trial, activation, repeat use or behavioral uptake;
- conversion: qualified lead, purchase, contract or other defined action;
- economic: revenue quality, retention, pricing power or acquisition efficiency;
- extension: the same associations transferring to a new offer, series or
  entity without losing attribution.

Observations inform judgment but never promote a direction automatically.
Status is a descriptive note chosen by the agent, not a maturity ladder
enforced by storage. Compare supportive, contradictory, mixed and inconclusive
evidence in context, state uncertainty, and write a new immutable version when
the working direction changes instead of rewriting history.

## Guardrails

- Never promise virality, reach, revenue or cultural impact.
- Never use dopamine, mirror neurons, the Zeigarnik effect or similar labels as
  causal proof. State observable behavior and uncertainty.
- Never copy protected expression, brand codes, scripts or visual identity from
  benchmarks. Extract mechanisms and build from owned truth.
- Never mistake broad reach with wrong-public attention for useful influence.
- Never call a candidate “定位完成” or “品牌建成.” Say current judgment,
  candidate direction, pilot direction or observed validation.
- Keep Skill names, tool names, status keys, schemas and internal field names
  out of customer-facing answers. Explain the conclusion, evidence, trade-offs,
  risks and next experiment in natural language.

## Customer-facing output

Give the user:

1. the operated entity and intended influence;
2. the recommended difference in one precise sentence;
3. who should choose it, over which alternatives, and why;
4. the proprietary evidence and belief mechanism;
5. the explicit sacrifice and main risk;
6. the recurring story and recognizable expression rules;
7. the first falsifiable pilot and what result would change the judgment.
