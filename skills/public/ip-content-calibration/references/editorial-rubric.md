# Editorial judgment rubric

Apply this internally and contextually. Do not expose a universal numeric score
or pretend the same weights fit every platform and format.

## Dimensions

1. **Business role** — the piece has one primary job: reach, trust, proof or
   conversion, and that job fits the current strategy.
2. **Audience tension** — it addresses a concrete desire, risk, contradiction,
   identity tension or unresolved question for the intended buyer.
3. **Promise and proof** — title, cover and opening make an honest promise that
   the body can substantiate with evidence, mechanism, example or demonstration.
4. **Hook-contract integrity** — the opening creates a reason to continue
   without bait-and-switch, fabricated urgency or unsupported authority.
5. **Progression** — each beat advances information, emotion, demonstration or
   decision. Remove repetition, throat-clearing and generic abstractions.
6. **Identity credibility** — voice, experience, boundaries, visuals and claims
   feel consistent with the actual subject and account.
7. **Format and platform fit** — duration, pacing, framing, captions, audio,
   interaction and distribution surface fit the consumption context without
   relying on stale folklore.
8. **Action continuity** — the ending delivers the promise and offers a
   proportionate next action that the profile and business can fulfill.

## Diagnostic order

When results disappoint, inspect the earliest measurable contract:

1. discovery and packaging;
2. initial stop/hold;
3. continued consumption;
4. trust and proof;
5. intent;
6. conversion and delivery.

Do not blame a later stage when an earlier contract failed. Pair every diagnosis
with the smallest next experiment that can distinguish competing explanations.
