# TikTok platform evidence

Last reviewed: 2026-07-30. Current Community Guidelines and in-product account
status outrank older architecture articles.

## Official evidence

- [How TikTok recommends For You videos](https://newsroom.tiktok.com/how-tiktok-recommends-videos-for-you?lang=en)
  describes user interactions, video information, lower-weight device/account
  settings, stronger signals such as completion, diversity controls, and that
  follower count or prior viral videos are not direct recommendation factors.
  It is a 2020 architecture explanation, not a current weight table.
- [TikTok recommendation help](https://support.tiktok.com/en/using-tiktok/exploring-videos/how-tiktok-recommends-content)
  is the current first-party recommendation entry.
- [Why a post was recommended](https://support.tiktok.com/en/getting-started/for-you/test-for-you)
  describes explanations such as similar interactions, recency, follows,
  regional popularity and video length.
- [TikTok Community Guidelines](https://www.tiktok.com/community-guidelines)
  is the current safety and recommendation-eligibility source.
- [Account and feature enforcement](https://www.tiktok.com/community-guidelines/en/accounts-features?cgversion=2025H2update&lang=en)
  describes how repeated For You-ineligible content can affect account
  discoverability and commercial-disclosure requirements.

## Diagnosis use

Inspect current For You eligibility, originality/duplicate status, commercial
disclosure, traffic sources, viewing behavior, interactions, audience, profile
actions and commercial outcomes.

Do not infer current weights, a fixed traffic-pool ladder or an account defect
from low views. Follower count and past virality do not guarantee or preclude
recommendation; content and audience response remain primary.
