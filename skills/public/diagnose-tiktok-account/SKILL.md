# Diagnose TikTok account

Use this for a concrete TikTok account. Read `references/platform-evidence.md` first.

1. Read `personal_ip_account_diagnostic_context`.
2. Treat observations as optional evidence. State missing or partial coverage,
   but do not let it prevent a provisional professional judgment.
3. Diagnose IP type, audience promise, desire/conflict, story and expression
   form before consumption, sharing, trust, intent and conversion.
4. Inspect For You eligibility, originality, disclosed commercial content,
   traffic sources and audience fit separately. Do not invent current weights.
5. Give a direct continue, adjust, or start-separately recommendation with its
   content/business rationale, platform constraint, unknowns and next content.

No fixed number of posts or complete funnel dataset is a prerequisite.
