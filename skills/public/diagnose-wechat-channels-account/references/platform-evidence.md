# WeChat Channels platform evidence

Last reviewed: 2026-07-30. Public official detail on current Video Accounts
ranking is limited. Treat that absence as an evidence boundary.

## Official evidence

- [Video Accounts Assistant](https://channels.weixin.qq.com/) is the current
  authenticated first-party source for account status, content, traffic,
  audience and creator analytics. Its observed fields, timestamps and coverage
  should be sealed as platform evidence.
- [Weixin Security Center](https://weixin110.qq.com/) is the first-party
  governance entry. Use the account's current in-product rule notices and
  eligibility status rather than a remembered rule.
- [Tencent engineering article on 看一看 ranking](https://cloud.tencent.com/developer/article/1659349)
  describes recall, coarse/fine ranking, mixing, user/content/social features,
  click and consumption objectives, and diversity for the 看一看 product. It is
  useful system-level background only; it is not proof of current Video
  Accounts weights.

## Diagnosis use

Collect the current creator console before judging Follow, Friends,
Recommended or other exposed traffic-source surfaces. Inspect sharing,
comments, profile actions and commercial outcomes as separate funnel evidence.

Do not claim that friend relations dominate current ranking, publish weights,
or call an account shadowbanned without current platform status. A weak post
normally diagnoses content or audience fit, not an account-level defect.
