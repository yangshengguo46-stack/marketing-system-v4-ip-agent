# YouTube platform evidence

Last reviewed: 2026-07-30. Use current YouTube Studio evidence for the target
channel and video.

## Official evidence

- [How YouTube recommendations work](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/16089387?hl=en)
  describes Home, Up Next and Shorts recommendations and signals such as
  viewing/search history, subscriptions, likes/dislikes, “not interested” and
  satisfaction surveys.
- [Creator recommendation-system guidance](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/16533387?hl=en-GB)
  emphasizes audience fit, long-term satisfaction and fresh per-video
  performance rather than permanent channel punishment.
- [Content performance: appeal, engagement and satisfaction](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/16559650?hl=en)
  describes packaging, promise delivery and audience response as creator
  diagnostic categories.
- [YouTube Search](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/16090438?hl=en)
  describes relevance, engagement and quality/expertise-authority-trust.
- [Search and discovery performance](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/11914225?hl=en-GB)
  states that no particular format is universally favored; performance and
  personalization matter.

## Diagnosis use

Separate Home, Up Next, Shorts and Search. Inspect impressions/appeal,
consumption/retention, satisfaction evidence, traffic sources, audience,
returning behavior, comments and commercial outcomes. Do not map one metric to
one psychological cause.

YouTube does not publish one fixed universal ranking formula. One poor video,
low channel views or posting frequency alone does not prove that a channel
must be replaced.
