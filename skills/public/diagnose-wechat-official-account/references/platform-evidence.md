# WeChat Official Account platform evidence

Last reviewed: 2026-07-30. Current in-product evidence is primary because
WeChat does not publish one universal Official Account ranking formula.

## Official evidence

- [WeChat Official Accounts Platform](https://mp.weixin.qq.com/) is the
  authenticated first-party source for subscriber delivery, article reads,
  sharing, source composition, user changes and account notices.
- [Weixin Security Center](https://weixin110.qq.com/) is the current governance
  entry. Collect the account's actual notice and eligibility status when a
  restriction is suspected.
- [Tencent engineering article on 看一看 ranking](https://cloud.tencent.com/developer/article/1659349)
  describes recall, ranking, mixing, multiple user/content/social features,
  click/consumption objectives and diversity for 看一看. It does not establish
  current weights for subscriber pushes, Search, chat or Moments distribution.

## Diagnosis use

Separate subscriber delivery, article opening, reading/consumption,
chat/Moments forwarding, Search, 看一看 and conversion paths. An open-rate
problem is not automatically a recommendation problem; a sharing win is not
automatically a sale.

Do not invent a single platform score, current weight table or “dead account”
threshold. Prefer content and funnel experiments unless first-party account
evidence proves a persistent structural issue.
