# Diagnose Instagram account

Use this for a concrete Instagram account. Read `references/platform-evidence.md` first.

1. Read `personal_ip_account_diagnostic_context`.
2. Use available observations as evidence, not as an admission checklist.
   Missing metrics lower confidence but do not stop analysis.
3. Diagnose IP type, audience promise, desire/conflict, story and expression
   form before examining comprehension, Reels consumption, sends/shares,
   trust, intent and conversion.
4. Distinguish Feed, Stories, Explore and Reels, and treat Account Status and
   recommendation eligibility as platform context rather than a content verdict.
5. Lead with continue, adjust, or start separately. Give the decisive reason,
   evidence and unknowns, then propose the next concrete content move.

Do not require a fixed sample size, a server-compiled verdict or complete
commercial data. Never invent recommendation weights.
