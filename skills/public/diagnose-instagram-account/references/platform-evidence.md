# Instagram platform evidence

Last reviewed: 2026-07-30. Live Account Status is the primary structural
evidence.

## Official evidence

- [Recommendation eligibility on Instagram](https://www.facebook.com/help/instagram/653964212890722?locale=en_GB)
  explains that professional accounts can inspect Account Status. Eligibility
  allows recommendation to non-followers but does not guarantee it; an
  ineligible account's content may still reach followers.
- [How Instagram recommendations work](https://www.facebook.com/help/instagram/313829416281232?locale=en_GB)
  describes personalized recommendations across Explore, Reels and Feed.
- [Instagram Best Practices](https://about.fb.com/news/2024/10/best-practices-education-hub-creators-instagram/)
  is the first-party creator education entry covering creation, engagement,
  reach, monetization and guidelines with personalized tips.
- [Original-content distribution update](https://about.fb.com/ltam/news/2024/05/ayudando-a-los-creadores-a-encontrar-nuevas-audiencias/)
  describes preference for original content over identical reposts and
  possible recommendation ineligibility for aggregators.
- [Instagram recommendation engineering](https://engineering.fb.com/2020/12/10/web/how-instagram-suggests-new-content/)
  provides architectural background on candidate sourcing and positive or
  negative interaction signals; it is not a current weight table.

## Diagnosis use

Separate Feed, Stories, Explore and Reels. Inspect Account Status, originality,
content inventory, surface traffic, viewing/interaction behavior, sends,
profile actions and commercial outcomes.

Do not infer live ranking weights or call low reach a shadowban. Eligibility is
a gate, not a promise of distribution; eligible but weak content remains a
content/audience diagnosis.
