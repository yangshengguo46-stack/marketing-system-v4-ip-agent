# Strategy judgment

Use this reference to compare strategies; do not expose it as a questionnaire
or customer-facing scorecard.

## Decision dimensions

Evaluate each candidate on:

1. **Paid problem** — a specific buyer already spends money, time or status to
   solve it.
2. **Credible proof** — the subject has experience, access, results, process or
   a transparent learning journey that can support the promise.
3. **Meaningful difference** — the approach, evidence, identity or delivery
   differs in a way the buyer can perceive.
4. **Offer fit** — product or service, price logic, delivery burden and gross
   margin fit the subject's real capacity.
5. **Content supply** — the subject can repeatedly produce useful reach, trust,
   proof and conversion material without inventing expertise.
6. **Conversion continuity** — profile, pinned content, lead capture, sales
   conversation, delivery and repeat/referral form one plausible path.
7. **Durability and risk** — platform dependence, rights, reputation, privacy,
   claims, operational fragility and burnout are bounded.

Reject candidates whose economics depend entirely on an unspecified future
audience, whose proof cannot support the promise, or whose content supply
requires a false persona.

## Evidence order

Prefer evidence in this order:

1. verified sales, delivery and customer outcomes;
2. repeated audience behavior and conversion signals;
3. the subject's existing work and demonstrated expertise;
4. comparable benchmark mechanisms;
5. interviews and explicit user statements;
6. untested hypotheses.

Do not average contradictions away. Name the conflict and design a pilot that
can resolve it.
