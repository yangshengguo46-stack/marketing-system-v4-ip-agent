# Audience response engineering

Design for observable viewer behavior without pretending to read brains or
predict virality. Treat cognitive, social and behavioral theories as design
hypotheses; connect each hypothesis to a script beat and a result that could be
observed after publication.

## Use four linked layers

### 1. Attention and comprehension

Create a fast, legible reason to continue:

- a meaningful expectation gap, anomaly or unresolved question;
- clear subject, stakes and changing information;
- sensory or formal contrast that serves the premise;
- an open loop that is honestly resolved, not a false “watch to the end”.

Novelty without comprehension produces confusion. Familiarity without a change
produces indifference.

### 2. Emotion and social meaning

Give the viewer a reason to care or take a position:

- recognizable lived tension or empathic identification;
- identity, role or relationship stakes without stereotyping;
- incompatible values, interpretations or interests that invite thought;
- safe vicarious release, relief, surprise, admiration or humor.

Disagreement must arise from a real tension. Do not manufacture harassment,
humiliation, dangerous acts or deceptive outrage for comments.

### 3. Sharing and cultural movement

Give the content a function outside the viewing session:

- useful language for something people struggle to express;
- knowledge, taste or perspective worth passing to another person;
- a compact, retellable premise;
- a behavior, format or question that can be adapted without copying identity.

“Social currency” is not permission to make unsupported authority claims.

### 4. Follow, save or action

Match the next action to the value delivered:

- save when future retrieval has a concrete use;
- follow when a repeatable series promise is visible;
- comment when the viewer has a meaningful choice, experience or interpretation;
- inquire or buy when the story makes the relevant problem, trust and next step
  clear without disguised guarantees.

## Build only the mechanisms the script needs

For each selected mechanism, write internally:

```text
viewer state -> script beat -> intended response -> observable signal -> failure sign
```

Use at most one primary mechanism per layer and let the story carry it. Do not
dump theory names into the customer answer or force every video to target every
metric. Platform distribution changes packaging and feedback, not the human
meaning at the center of the content.

## Integrate with story

- Use verified benchmark patterns as evidence, not as a script template.
- Use desire, action, conflict and state change to make attention devices part
  of the event rather than decorations around it.
- Preserve the IP subject's recognizable meaning and reason to choose.
- Distinguish observed source facts, interpretation and creative hypotheses.

Never promise a view count, completion rate or conversion. A plausible mechanism
earns a test, not a verdict.
